A History of 1049 Keith Avenue, Berkeley

Paul Theodore Beygrau (1871-1935) was a designer, artist, and inventor, born in England who spent most of his life in North America.

Marie Rosalie Schaaf, an Alsatian woodcarver.

Agnes Emily Hoyle (1883-1974), a San Francisco lawyer.

John W. Winkler (1894-1979), an Austrian born American etcher.

By the late '60s, 1049 Keith was rented out to Berkeley graduate students, including David Minkus (1944-2024), a sociologist, Walter N Hittelman, now a cancer researcher, and his wife, Michele M Beaudoin. Judy “Gumbo” Clavir, a red diaper baby from Toronto, who had recently arrived in the Bay Area. Stewart Edward “Stew” Albert (1939-2006), one of the founders of the Yippies. Eldridge Cleaver (1935-1998), Minister of Information for the Black Panthers.

Sobky (السبكي) family.
